HOW WE THINK ABOUT WEATHER

Not just another
weather app.

We want to understand weather from the ground up—through historical evidence, transparent methods, and honest measurement.

Transparent third-party model inputs · Atmos verification underway
01 ↗

Look back to look ahead.

For the experimental fallback, we pull daily historical temperature, rainfall, and wind measurements from NASA POWER, reaching back to 1981 for each selected location.

02 ↗

Find the local pattern.

An experimental seasonal-analog calculation combines the entire available local archive with recent departures from normal. Live short-range forecasts use Open-Meteo numerical weather models; our experimental historical model remains a fallback.

03 ↗

Show our homework.

Forecast dates, information cutoffs, model version, and research limitations stay visible. Our independent global forecasting model is still under development.

THE RESEARCH LAB

Built on curiosity.
Measured with honesty.

Our historical research tested four forecast lead times on 1,000 unseen dates around the world. These are internal experimental scores against retrospective NASA gridded data—not accuracy percentages, and not validation of this live prototype model.
